

# Pravin Hota — Premium Law Firm Website

A BCI-compliant, authoritative legal portfolio website with 8 pages, a mandatory disclaimer gate, and a refined dark blue + gold design system.

---

## Design System Setup
- **Color palette**: Oxford Blue (#011B2F), Prussian Blue (#002A45), Indigo Dye (#003F66), Mustard (#FFD358), Selective Yellow (#FFB915)
- **Typography**: Google Fonts — Playfair Display for headings, Inter for body text, off-white (#F5F5F5) text
- **Tone**: Minimal, authoritative, corporate — no flashy animations, only subtle fade transitions and premium spacing

---

## 1. Disclaimer Gate (First Screen)
- Full-screen modal overlay on Oxford Blue background
- Centered content box with the exact BCI-mandated disclaimer text provided
- Gold "I AGREE" button — website content is hidden/inaccessible until accepted
- Agreement stored in session so it persists during the visit

## 2. Sticky Navigation Bar
- 8 menu items: Home, About Us, Team/People, Practice Areas, Services, Contract Management, Thought Leadership, Contact Us
- Transparent on top, transitions to Prussian Blue (#002A45) on scroll
- Thin gold bottom border accent
- Mobile hamburger menu

## 3. Home Page
- **Hero**: Dark background with elegant tagline ("Integrity. Precision. Legal Excellence."), formal subtext, gold divider
- **About Snapshot**: Brief introduction section linking to full About page
- **Practice Areas Grid**: 6 cards previewing key areas
- **Thought Leadership Preview**: 2-3 latest article cards
- **Contact CTA**: Non-aggressive, formal invitation to get in touch

## 4. About Us Page
- Professional biography, education, bar enrollment details
- Philosophy and values section
- Vertical timeline layout for career milestones

## 5. Team / People Page
- Clean card grid for 2-5 team members
- Circular photo placeholders, name, designation, short neutral bio
- No promotional language

## 6. Practice Areas Page
- Grid layout with elegant line icons (gold)
- Areas: Corporate Law, Civil Litigation, Criminal Law, Arbitration, Contract Advisory, Regulatory Compliance
- Hover: background shifts to Indigo Dye with gold accent underline

## 7. Services Page
- Structured sections: Legal Advisory, Litigation Support, Documentation, Due Diligence, Representation
- Elegant typography with clear descriptions

## 8. Contract Management Page
- Dedicated corporate-style page
- Sections: Drafting, Reviewing, Negotiation Support, Risk Mitigation, Lifecycle Management
- Minimal gold line icons as visual accents

## 9. Thought Leadership Page
- Blog-style card layout with hardcoded sample articles
- Dark blue cards with gold metadata (date, category)
- Article detail view for each post

## 10. Contact Us Page
- Minimal form: Name, Email, Phone, Message (display only, no backend)
- Office address section with Google Maps embed (DLF Cybercity Bhubaneswar)
- Footer disclaimer repeating BCI compliance note
